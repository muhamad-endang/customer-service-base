# Customer Service QC

Before final client-facing response, verify:

## Accuracy
- Answers actual unresolved question.
- Correct client/project.
- No invented fact.
- No stale price overriding newer data.
- No project example generalized universally.

## Authority
- CS is authorized.
- Required Developer/Management validation exists.
- No unapproved discount, deadline, feature, server performance, publication approval, unlimited revision, or free change-scope promise.

## Communication
- Natural and human.
- To the point.
- Non-technical understandable.
- Appropriate to lifecycle.
- Not defensive.
- No internal wording exposed.
- Useful CTA when needed.

## Commercial
- Truthful value framing.
- No fake urgency/scarcity.
- No unnecessary discount.
- Closing language proportional to intent.

## Scope / Risk
- Important limitations preserved.
- Change requests not silently accepted as free scope.
- Technical uncertainty not presented as certainty.
